\textbf{Shared visual responses, their restoration, and task outcomes.}
(a) Clean and perturbed observations of the same physical state (main camera shown) pass through frozen $\pi_{0.5}$ with the same policy noise. After action-expert blocks L1, L9, and L18 we record the response, $\Delta H_\ell = H^{p}_\ell - H^{c}_\ell$. On held-out tasks and families, 16 shared directions per layer retain 36.5--83.6\% of its energy, against at most 10.2\% for the controls (Fig.~\ref{fig:token-structure}). Restoration copies clean values along these directions back after L9.
(b) Restoration versus the unedited perturbed run (simultaneous 95\% intervals; Table~\ref{tab:restoration}). The distance to the clean first five translational commands falls by 24\% under Camera and 5\% under Sensor Noise. Estimated success gains are 1.85 and 3.33 percentage points; both intervals include zero.
(c) Under Sensor Noise, $\pi_0$ moves less after five controls than a clean rerun, yet loses 14.3 points of success (adjusted 95\% interval; Fig.~\ref{fig:behavior}).
